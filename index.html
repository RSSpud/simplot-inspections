Weighted summary

Weighted by sample pounds, for the reports that match the filters above. Click a row to summarize that group.

Weighting: each report's value × its sample weight ÷ total sample weight. Specific gravity is weighted the same way. Bruise free and US #1 are reported by Simplot on the after-peel sample, size and tare on the grading sample, so they have different bases. Crop year is read from each report. Origin is the field named in Simplot's Source line; grower is matched to it by crop year from the Active Field Years export, and shows 'Unmatched' when that year's field list has no match; a grower marked 'Origin map (confirmed)' comes from a map Rhett confirmed for storage locations. Contract number is read from each report. A blank value means the report has no data for it (for example, no fry color samples), and it is left out of averages. Averages across crop years mix seasons, so with several crop years selected the summary tiles show each year separately, earliest first, separated by a slash. Sugar ends is the Attribute Summary value; sunburn/green is the Attribute Summary sunburn %. In the filters, picking several values in one list shows reports matching any of them; different lists narrow each other.
